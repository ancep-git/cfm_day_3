DPI-HT-01 · practice for the material-decisions test

Material decisions quiz

Each round takes one of your 25 material decisions and works it like the test: sort the evidence, name the finance rule, answer two short explanation questions, then trace one changed fact through profit, cash and the balance sheet.

How the Finance Reference asks you to separate evidence

Reference §10: "Prefer bank and signed third-party evidence over unsupported management claims. Preserve competing interpretations, quantify their effect, state confidence and make the final judgment yourself."

StrongBank records and signed or third-party documents. E00 board order, E02 bank, E04 signed contracts, E06 supplier invoices, E09 loan agreement, owner card and counsel, E11 confirmations after the takeover.
MediumThe company's own records: useful, but check them against strong evidence. E03 CRM, E05 warehouse count, E07 payroll, E08 asset register.
WeakUnsupported management claims and pressure. Use them only as evidence of override, never as the number. E01 management spreadsheet, E10 emails and WhatsApp.
