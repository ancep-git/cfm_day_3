How to answer any changed-fact question

Every fact touches three different "boxes". Ask about each box separately. They often move differently.

ProfitDid we earn something or use up something in Jan–Aug? Earned = up. Used up, lost or probably owed = down. Money moving is not the question here.
CashDid money actually go in or out of the bank before 31 Aug? If not, cash does not change, even if profit does. The bank export shows 60,000.
Balance sheetWhat do we own (assets) and what do we owe (liabilities) on 31 Aug? Equity = assets − liabilities. Any profit change lands in equity too, so it always balances.

The recipe

  1. Name the rule: sale on delivery? tool or cost? owed or not? owner's money?
  2. Profit: up or down, by how much, and which line (revenue, cost of sales, an expense).
  3. Cash: did the bank move before 31 Aug? If yes, which section (operating, investing or financing)?
  4. Balance sheet: which asset or liability moves? Then check that equity moves by the same amount as profit (or by the owner's withdrawal).
Model answer: "Profit goes down by 10,000, because it is a repair that only kept the machine working. Cash does not change, because it was already paid; it just moves from investing to operating. On the balance sheet, PPE falls by 10,000 and equity falls by 10,000, so it still balances."
